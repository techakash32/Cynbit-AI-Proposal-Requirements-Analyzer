# Synthetic evaluation brief — business analytics dashboard

This is synthetic assessment input only, not a customer record, benchmark, accuracy claim, or seeded application data.

A business wants an internal dashboard that combines CRM, billing, and product usage data. Leaders should see revenue, retention, pipeline, and activation trends with date and segment filters. Analysts need drill-down, exports, scheduled email delivery, and a metric-definition catalog. Data should refresh daily and show freshness and source lineage. Access must follow department permissions.

## Assessment expectations (not measured results)
- Expected requirement count: 10–16 atomic requirements after decomposition.
- Known gaps to look for: canonical metric definitions, source ownership, refresh SLA, historical backfill, row-level access rules, export retention, and reconciliation/acceptance thresholds.
- Representative risks to consider: metric disagreement, stale or missing data, permission leakage, warehouse cost growth, and misleading trends from incomplete history.
