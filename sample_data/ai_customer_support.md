# Synthetic evaluation brief — AI customer support application

This is synthetic assessment input only, not a customer record, benchmark, accuracy claim, or seeded application data.

A support team wants a web application that answers questions from an approved knowledge base, suggests replies, and routes unresolved cases to a human queue. Agents need conversation history, citations to the knowledge base, feedback controls, and role-based access. The business expects English at launch and wants a review workflow before an answer is sent. The system should integrate with an existing ticketing system and capture response latency and resolution outcomes.

## Assessment expectations (not measured results)
- Expected requirement count: 8–12 atomic requirements after decomposition.
- Known gaps to look for: knowledge-base ownership/freshness, escalation SLA, PII retention, model/provider constraints, ticketing API limits, and evaluation acceptance criteria.
- Representative risks to consider: unsupported or stale answers, prompt injection through customer content, privacy leakage, provider outage/latency, and human-review bottlenecks.
